How the Monday Special works

  • Sunday at noon Eastern, start the special for the upcoming Monday through Wednesday if all three nights are vacant.
  • Set a three-night minimum for Monday arrivals. Tuesday and Wednesday keep their PriceLabs rates and minimum stays.
  • Monday at 2:15 AM Eastern, reread Hostaway rates after the 2 AM PriceLabs sync and refresh the discount. A rate still carrying the Sunday discount uses its original price as the baseline.
  • Monday at 5 PM Eastern, stop the special and restore the Monday price and minimum stay when those values still match the automation’s last write. Later changes from PriceLabs or staff are preserved.
{% else %}

How the Thursday Special works

  • At the selected time on Thursday, start the special for Thursday through Saturday if all three nights are vacant.
  • Set a three-night minimum for Thursday arrivals. Friday and Saturday keep their PriceLabs rates and minimum stays.
  • The scheduled run applies once per owner. PriceLabs can update the rates on its next sync.
{% endif %}

We calculate the discount using the total Hostaway rate for all three nights and deduct the entire savings from {{ special_day }}. For example, 20% off a $600 three-night total saves $120 on {{ special_day }}. The three-night arrival minimum ties that savings to a booking that fills all three nights.
